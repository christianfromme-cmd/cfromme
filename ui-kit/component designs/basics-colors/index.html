Colors

Primary Colors

  • $color-rwe-blue
    #1d4477
    Variants: -75, -50, -25
  • $gradient-green
    #00a19f

RWE Blue is the main brand colour. The colour is used both in the logo and as the colour for the copy. If necessary, the colours can also be used in strengths of 75%, 50% and 25%, e.g. in illustrations or infographics.

The secondary colours complement the primary colours. They provide vibrant accents and ensure quick orientation. They are primarily used for structuring information (e.g. in tables and graphics).
