    Explore everything we offer

    We know that it pays off to invest in the future. And that’s why we make a point to ensure that employees are well looked after. Everyone is different, which is why we have a vast range of benefits. After all, the energy of our teams drives the energy supply of tomorrow.

    Benefits & rewards

    Financial benefits

    Competitive salaries and remuneration packages get us the right talent to transform the industry and grow. And our carefully considered pension options help you earn and save for a future that’s secure and worth looking forward to.

    Always learning

    At RWE, you’ll have access to an impressive array of opportunities for personal development through training, mentorship and education. It’s an opportunity you can't miss to customise and develop your future – and impact ours.

    Working to suit your circumstances

    Flexible working hours, mobile working, as well as parent-child offices and in-house day nurseries at some sites help you balance the demands of both professional and personal worlds. We also offer parental leave and additional support through parental leave coaching to empower you during this important phase of your life.

    Healthy mind and body

    Health management at RWE covers more than just your standard health package – company medical facilities, counselling, and options for various sports and social clubs keep us happy, healthy and connected.

    Committed to giving back

    We believe in getting involved and giving back to our communities. Whether locally, nationally or globally, we support countless projects for education, culture and social initiatives.

    Occupational safety

    Occupational safety and health protection are geared towards international standards and are high priority – whether it’s in a wind farm, in a mining area, on campus or in your home office.

    Learning & Development

    Your impact drives our energy capabilities, so we offer a wide range of holistic, hands-on and tailored ways to grow.

    Onboarding

    A good onboarding process is particularly important to us to make you feel an integral part of #TeamRWE right from the start.

    Hybrid working

    We understand change – and work culture is transforming around the world.

    With digitalisation, globalisation, individualisation and sustainability, it can be easy and efficient to build greater trust with each other and have a more flexible work life with a hybrid model.

    It might not work for every role at every location however our guidelines help bringing hybrid working to live.

    We build on trust and bank flexibility

    We work digitally and in person

    We work globally and locally – nationally and internationally

    We develop ourselves

    We keep an eye on people and resources.
