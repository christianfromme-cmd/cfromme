    Growing together through our inclusive culture

    At RWE, we highly value Diversity, Equity and Inclusion and consider these values to be fundamental to our corporate success. For us an inclusive culture means creating a working environment in which all employees feel valued, respected and empowered to contribute their unique perspectives and talents. For applicants, this means becoming part of a company that not only recognises differences, but understands them as enrichment.

    Meet our global team

    We’re proud of #TeamRWE, which serves as the heart of our company. Our colourful and diverse workforce reflects society and enriches our corporate culture. It’s important to us that all employees are given the same opportunities and chances to develop and fully utilise their talents.

    Together, we create an inclusive working environment in which every voice counts and diversity is recognised as a strength.

    What makes us special? See for yourself!

    Inclusive working environment

    RWE actively embraces diversity across many dimensions, fostering an inclusive environment where different perspectives and talents come together to drive innovation. We actively support diverse teams, knowing that a variety of experiences leads to the best solutions. Equal opportunities are a key priority for us - we promote an environment where cultural diversity thrives. At RWE, employees from all backgrounds are encouraged to contribute their unique strengths and perspectives. Learn more about our commitment to diversity here!

    Fostering diversity

    Gender

    Gender equality in the workplace is one of our top priorities. We’re aiming to strengthen the share of women in leadership, with a goal of 30% in our core business by 2030. The initiatives we’re implementing across the group ensure that we develop and grow a fair and equitable workplace.

    Our RWE Women’s Networks promote the free and open exchange of ideas, experiences and career advice. And together, they’re pushing for greater empowerment of women in the energy sector. Recently, we celebrated Women in Engineering Day, with events held across the UK to promote the amazing work of female engineers around the world.

    LGBT*IQ

    Everyone is a valued member of the team, whatever their sexual orientation or gender identity. We believe all employees have the right to express their gender or sexual identity in the workplace and should feel comfortable doing so. Diverse teams deliver better results — plus broader, healthier perspectives. We are all accountable for ensuring all employees feel welcome and free to be who they are.

    It’s why we’ve created the LGBT*IQ & Friends network and we’re passionate supporters of Pride, celebrating in style in 2024. We’ve also created informative podcasts on the LGBT*IQ community at RWE and how to become a better ally and supporter.

    Generational diversity

    Our unique perspectives make us stronger. It’s why we strive for a multi- generational mix of employees, and support lifelong learning for every employee of every age. A transfer of knowledge between new and experienced employees creates an inclusive culture – as well as introduces the next generation of employees into the workplace. Not only does this emphasise the importance of diversity of thought, it also guarantees our long-term success.

    Disability

    Everyone, with or without a disability, has a basic right to feel included and treated respectfully. At RWE, we know disability should not be a barrier to achievement or performance at work – it’s why we support multiple forms of accessibility. We work to ensure everyone gets equitable treatment in a positive and understanding working environment. And strengthening a culture that is open and sensibly accessible to everyone on every level.

    Culture, ethnicity and religion

    We respect the uniqueness of all people – no matter what your background or belief is, you’re welcome at RWE.

    RWE is a truly international company – in its approach, its methods and its vision. With over 80 locations around the world, our workforce is drawn from a vast range of cultures, ethnicities and religions. That’s why we provide safe spaces where different religious customs and practices can be honoured and respected.

    We’re also signed up to the “Charta der Vielfalt” (“Diversity Charter”) – one of Germany’s largest corporate initiatives, with over 5,900 members and partner initiatives across Germany. This charter promotes the recognition, appreciation and integration of diversity, equity and inclusion into business culture. Something we’re overwhelmingly proud of.

    The Power of all Voices

    ‘The Power of All Voices’ is our commitment to creating an inclusive environment where every perspective matters. By ensuring that introverts and extroverts are equally heard, we strengthen collaboration, improve decision-making, and unlock the full potential of our teams.

    At RWE, your voice has impact from the start. By sharing your ideas and perspectives, you help shape how we work together and contribute to solutions that drive our business and the future of energy forward.

    Employee resource networks

    Several employee networks are available at RWE for you. Make an impact on topics that are important to you.

    And across our diverse networks, we know there will be people facing the same challenges and joys, trials and celebrations. So, we’ve encouraged those people to come together – creating powerful communities who can inspire, support and champion each other in their daily work and beyond.

    Including You @ RWE

    A broad community of employees committed to building a fair, open working culture.

    The Including You @ RWE Community offers regular opportunities to connect, collaborate and exchange.

    Whether its during regular monthly calls or in team meetings, community members raise awareness of how to communicate and collaborate in an inclusive way across many geographies and operating companies.

    Women’s Network

    Developing successful careers with collaborating, connecting and providing support.

    The RWE Women’s Network is a self-organised network in which over 1000 women collaborate, connect and provide support for one another to develop successful careers.

    They engage women from across the entire company, hosting a yearly conference and offering working sessions about current challenges of the energy economy and leadership.

    LGBT*IQ & Friends

    Our internal LGBT*IQ & Friends Network unites LGBT*IQ employees and their allies.

    The community’s goal is to raise awareness both internally and externally – and foster a working environment where everyone is welcome to be as they are.

    This is just one of the great, safe spaces at RWE. Regularly promoting greater understanding within the wider business, they educate and inspire employees around the globe on topics such as coming out at work and LGBT*IQ rights. 

    Empower @ RWE

    Raising awareness on the topics disability, neurodiversity and mental health.

    Empower is the name of our fledgling employee network raising awareness on the topics disability, neurodiversity and mental health. It is available for allies, as well as those who have disabilities.

    Frequent networking sessions are held to give support and guidance to those who need it. We’re always looking for ways we can empower employees – the more we learn, the greater action we can take.

    Rachel Disney
    Head of Diversity, RWE AG
