    Global presence – your opportunity

    You may already know that our colleagues at RWE speak many languages. But what do you actually know about RWE's locations?

    Did you know that we are present in more than 20 countries and that people of more than 80 nationalities work for RWE all over the world?

    From here you can explore some of our locations.

    Take a look insight

    Austin (Texas), USA

    With its modern design and east-side location our Austin office offers all the charm that this capital city has to offer. Employees have access to on-site parking and a fitness center, fully stocked snack bar, complimentary soft drinks and plenty of recreational space; indoors and outdoors to spend time with fellow colleagues.

    Interested in joining a growing team of renewable energy experts shaping the future?

    RWE Careers in the Americas

    Find jobs in Austin

    Bellevue (AI Lab), USA

    In 2024, we opened our RWE AI Research Laboratory in Bellevue, aiming to become a global leader in developing forecasting models. Why here? Because the Greater Seattle area is a growing hotspot for artificial intelligence and renewable energy. Bellevue brings together top tech talent, a strong research environment, a vibrant startup scene and many companies driving the energy transition – the perfect conditions to boost innovation and collaboration.

    RWE Careers in the Americas

    Discover jobs in the U.S.

    Chicago, USA

    RWE’s Chicago office is in the center of it all in the bustling city. Steps away from the scenic riverwalk, lively restaurants and multiple transportation hubs (Union Station and Oglivie Transportation Center), our office is proud to be in a LEED Platinum and Energy Start-certified building. Employees have access to onsite parking, a fitness center and cafeteria, in addition to complementary in-office, snacks and beverages. The perfect office for any professional looking to experience all Chicago has to offer.

    RWE Careers in the Americas

    Explore job options in Chicago

    New York City, USA

    Since 2011, RWE Supply & Trading has been represented in New York City by its subsidiary RWE Trading Americas. In order to support RWE’s ambitious expansion plans in North America, the New York-based team moved into a larger and more suitable office space within the Rockefeller Center in midtown Manhattan in spring 2024.

    RWE Careers in the Americas

    Discover jobs in New York City

    Oakland, USA

    RWE Americas is located in Oakland's vibrant Uptown neighborhood. The new building is a LEED-certified high rise next to Lake Merritt. The office is located on twenty seventh floor and features a view of the entire San Francisco Bay.

    RWE Careers in the Americas

    Discover jobs in Oakland

    San Diego, USA

    Centrally located in sunny downtown San Diego, commutable by foot, trolley (just three blocks away) or car, the San Diego office offers great views, on-site parking, complimentary snacks and drinks, as well as local events access offered through the building management company. Surrounded by bustling restaurants and fragrant coffee roasters, it’s the perfect place to get work done while enjoying the California sunshine.

    RWE Careers in the Americas

    Join #TeamRWE in San Diego

    Beijing, China

    In 2016, RWE successfully entered the Chinese market and since then has grown in its activities. Today, the RWE Group is mainly present through its wholly-owned subsidiary RWE Supply & Trading (Shanghai) Co. Ltd., with offices in both Beijing and Shanghai.

    RWE Careers in Asia

    Explore job options in China

    Shanghai, China

    RWE Supply & Trading (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. is based in Shanghai – a key hub for energy and trade in Asia. The office focuses on international and domestic energy transactions within China, while exploring opportunities in conventional and renewable energy markets. It also shares expertise in trading and asset optimisation, seeking growth in line with local regulations.

    RWE Careers in Asia

    Explore job options in China

    Copenhagen, Denmark

    RWE’s renewables business has been active in the Nordic market since 2018, with around 170 employees. Our central office in Copenhagen has 100 employees and is located close to the City Hall Square, Tivoli and the main railway station, surrounded by canals, cycle paths, museums and restaurants.

    Interested in joining a growing team of renewable energy experts shaping the future?

    Find jobs in Copenhagen

    Essen (Headquarter), Germany

    Essen has been home to RWE for more than 125 years. We're proud of our history and excited about driving the energy revolution and becoming a leader in renewables.

    Curious about working at the RWE Campus in the heart of the Rhine-Ruhr region?

    Find jobs in Essen.

    Find out more about the RWE Campus in Essen

    Hamburg, Germany

    In April 2021, our renewables business moved to Drehbahn. Near Planten & Blomen and Alster Lake, the 1920s listed building offers modern offices, a roof terrace, a piazza, and a green wall.

    Find jobs in Hamburg.

    Curious about working and living in Hamburg with #TeamRWE?

    Find out more about RWE in Hamburg

    Paris, France

    In October 2021, RWE Renouvelable France moved to new offices in Clichy, west of the périphérique motorway. Our teams of +250 employees are together in a bespoke office space to drive the #energytransition.

    RWE Careers in France

    Find jobs in Paris

    Mumbai, India

    RWE Supply & Trading (India) Private Limited, a wholly owned RWE Supply & Trading subsidiary, opened its office in November 2013. Located in India’s financial capital of Mumbai, RWEST IPL consolidates RWEST’s Indian commodity trading and marketing activities. As part of RWEST’s growth strategy, RWEST IPL focuses on building a commodities business in India.

    RWE Careers in Asia

    Discover job opportunities in India

    Jakarta, Indonesia

    Our office in Jakarta is small, but crucial. RWE Supply & Trading (RWEST) aims to be a leader in Indonesia's competitive energy market. The team is responsible for commercial trading, business analysis, finance, tax, government reporting and office management. 

    RWE Careers in Asia

    Discover job opportunities in Jakarta

    Tokyo, Japan

    Our Tokyo office plays a key role in RWE's international growth ambitions in Asia, focusing on both RWE Renewables Japan G.K. and RWE Supply & Trading Japan Inc. in Japan.

    Centrally located in Tokyo, our office is surrounded by excellent restaurants - perfect for a visit. Are you interested in joining our growing team?

    RWE Careers in Asia

    Explore jobs opportunities in Tokyo

    Singapore

    The Asia Pacific region continues to be a strong focus area for RWE Supply & Trading. That’s why our colleagues in Singapore recently moved to a newer, larger office in Singapore Land Tower, located in the middle of Singapore's bustling financial district. This RWE office was officially opened in September 2024.

    RWE Careers in Asia

    Join #TeamRWE in Singapore

    London, United Kingdom

    Our London office at The Northcliffe, 28 Tudor Street brings together colleagues from various RWE Group companies. Located in the heart of the City of London, just moments from Fleet Street and the River Thames, the office offers modern workspaces as well as meeting and collaboration spaces. Its open design encourages creativity, communication and the exchange of ideas. A wide selection of cafés, restaurants and bars can be found nearby. A rooftop terrace overlooking the London Eye and the London skyline offers an inspiring place to take a break, enjoy the view or connect with colleagues.

    RWE Careers in United Kingdom

    Join our vibrant London team!

    Swindon, United Kingdom

    The Trigonos building in Swindon is home to more than 1,000 employees from various RWE Group companies, including employees from different RWE Group companies, and fosters a vibrant networking environment.

    Our recently refurbished office has a great canteen, free on-site parking, quiet rooms and a free gym with yoga, pilates and HIIT classes. On-site clubs cater for running, football, netball, golf and cricket enthusiasts, providing a dynamic workplace for productive days and networking with colleagues.

    RWE Careers in United Kingdom

    Find jobs in Swindon
