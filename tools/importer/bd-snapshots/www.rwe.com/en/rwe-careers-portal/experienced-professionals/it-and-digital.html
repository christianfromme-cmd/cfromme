    #TeamRWE insights
    Current roles

    Solving problems, making connections

    You keep us connected and running. As a qualified IT pro, you’ll work in dynamic self-organising teams using the latest technologies to respond to rapidly changing requirements.

    Whether you’re operating and upgrading the data backbone of a major division, creating next-gen digital tools to streamline processes or helping departments harmonise their tech stacks – your work is vital to everything we do.

    Leading the way in IT & Digital

    Problem-solving is among humankind’s greatest pleasures, and it’s one you’ll experience daily at RWE. As part of a close-knit Central IT team with a collaborative, feedback-oriented culture, you’ll also find plenty of opportunities for growth, independence and initiative.

    • This role is all about creating real competitive advantage for RWE and driving IT transformation through digitalisation, data analytics and process automation. Whether you’re assisting with group-wide applications, including SAP and non-SAP solutions, or managing our Solutions & Services End-2-End (design to deliver) as IT-Consultants and Process Experts – you’ll be in alignment with the business as our go-to expert for SAP competencies and tech impact, making sure, that our IT Services are delivered in the required quality, time, and cost.

      Search jobs

    • In the IT Infrastructure department, you’ll be responsible for global services across all RWE segments, providing stable, scalable, reliable and cost-effective services and innovations – all together forming the foundation of our technology systems.

      This is a crucial role in driving our business success, and we offer extensive opportunities for personal development and ongoing experiences through critical IT change programmes and operations. This team continually adapts and evolves its technologies and processes to meet the rapid change in demands of RWE businesses. We deliver modern Workplace Technology to all end users despite of the location, integrating Network & Communication Services with Enterprise Service Management processes and tools from our Cloud & Data Centers. Our world-class team leverages cutting-edge technologies to ensure business delivery in the fast-growing energy market globally.

      Search jobs

    • The IT Strategy & Operational Excellence team is responsible for the RWE group wide IT strategy. Working with us means managing strategic and operational IT projects across RWE AG or supporting projects in the different RWE companies. Your impact will drive the continuous improvement of group-wide initiatives, like SLA management, governance processes and risk management, and you’ll have the opportunity to oversee the lifecycles of software licensing and related projects. From here, your expertise will strengthen us to build a modern energy world.

      Search jobs

    • If you take up a role within the Enterprise Architecture team you’ll be responsible for

      • Architectural Governance: Aligning IT architecture with RWE’s strategic objectives to support long-term goals supporting governance structures to ensure compliance with architectural standards and best practices.
      • Technology Roadmapping: Developing and enhancing technology roadmaps to leverage emerging technologies for business objectives.
      • Innovation and Optimization: Scouting innovation and optimizing IT architecture to meet evolving business needs.
      • Stakeholder Collaboration: Collaborating with business leaders, RWE’s operating companies, and external partners to align architectural decisions with business requirements.
